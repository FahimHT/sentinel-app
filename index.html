Sentinel

A dashcam for Android.

Sentinel turns a phone into a dashcam. Start a recording, put the phone away or lock it, and it keeps recording video to the phone's gallery until you stop it or the storage runs low.

What it does

  • Records from the front or back camera, at resolutions from 360p to 1080p, with or without sound.
  • Keeps recording with the app in the background, the screen locked or the screen off. A notification shows for as long as it records, with a button to stop.
  • Saves the recording in short segments, so a long drive is a series of files rather than one that a crash could ruin, to the Movies/Sentinel folder on the phone or its SD card. They appear in the gallery like any other video.
  • Stops on its own when free space runs low, and never deletes footage to make room.
  • Can play a sound when a recording stops, so you know without looking at the screen.

What it does not do

  • No account, no server, no cloud upload. The app has no internet access at all.
  • No analytics, no telemetry, no advertising.
  • No location is recorded.
  • It never starts recording on its own. Every recording is started by you.

Everything it records stays on your phone; the privacy policy has the details.

Requirements

  • Android 12 or newer.
  • A phone with a camera and a microphone.

Recording video and sound of other people is regulated in many places. Check the law where you drive, and use the app while driving at your own discretion.
