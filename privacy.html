Privacy policy

Sentinel, by Owl Software · last updated 7 October 2026

Sentinel has no servers and no internet access. Nothing it records is sent to the developer or to anyone else, and there is no account to create. Everything below describes data that stays on your phone.

What the app records

While a recording runs, Sentinel records video from the camera you choose and, unless you turn sound off, audio from the microphone. Recordings are saved as video files in the Movies/Sentinel folder on the phone, or on its SD card if you choose that in Settings. Like any video in that folder, they appear in your gallery, and other apps you have given access to your videos can see them.

The app records only while you have started a recording. It never starts one by itself, and a notification is shown for the whole time it records.

It also writes diagnostic log files to its own storage on the phone, to make faults reproducible. They describe what the app did — recordings starting and stopping, settings, errors — and contain no video, no audio and no location.

What it deliberately does not do

  • No location is recorded. The app does not ask for location access.
  • Nothing is uploaded. The app does not request internet access, so it cannot send anything off the phone.
  • There is no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking of any kind.

Sharing debug data

Settings has a Collect Debug Data button for reporting a problem. It builds a zip of the log files, the app's settings and a list of the recording file names and sizes — not the recordings themselves — and opens Android's share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you choose where it goes.

Permissions, and why each exists

  • Camera — to record video.
  • Microphone — to record sound with the video. Turn sound off in Settings and the microphone is not used.
  • Notifications — for the notification shown while recording, which also carries the button to stop.
  • Foreground service — to keep recording with the app in the background or the screen off. Android requires the notification above while it does.

Deleting your data

Owl Software holds no copy of your data. Everything the app records is on your phone, and you delete it yourself:

  1. One recording: delete it in your gallery or file manager, like any video.
  2. All recordings: Settings, then Delete All Recordings. This deletes the recordings this installation of the app made. Any left from an earlier installation are not visible to it, and can be deleted from the gallery.
  3. Settings and logs: uninstall the app, which removes them.

Uninstalling does not delete your recordings, on purpose: footage of an incident should not disappear because the app was removed. They stay in Movies/Sentinel until you delete them.

What is kept: nothing else. Deleted data is gone at once, and no other copy exists anywhere.

Recording other people

Recordings can include other people, number plates and conversations in the car. Laws on recording video and sound differ between countries; it is up to you to use the app where and how the law allows.

Children

The app is a dashcam for drivers and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. No change will make the app send data anywhere without asking you first.
